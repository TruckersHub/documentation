---
title: Tracker Uninstallation
---

You must perform the following steps in order to remove the TruckersHub client:

1\. Start **Run** by pressing the **Windows key and the r letter** on your keyboard at the same time.

2\. Next, enter **'%appdata%'**.

3\. Next, locate and access the **TruckersHub Tracker** folder.

4\. When you locate and open the **uninstall** file, your TruckersHub Tracker will be completely removed.
